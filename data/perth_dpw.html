Department of Public Works

The City of Perth Amboy Department of Public Works helps keep our streets, neighborhoods, and public spaces clean, safe, and well maintained.

Use the information below to find service guidelines, schedules, and contact information for the appropriate office.

Before placing items at the curb or bringing materials to a drop-off location, please review the applicable guidelines, including accepted items, service hours, and any restrictions.

For questions or to report a Public Works concern, call 732-826-2010 or use the contact information listed below.
