Division of Recycling & Solid Waste

  • Home

  • Division of Recycling & Solid Waste


Locations to dispose safely your lithium-ion batteries


The Division of Recycling & Solid Waste is responsible for overseeing various sanitation operations in order to promote sustainability and responsible waste management throughout town. It provides the following services:

  • Bulk Pick-Up (curbside bulk trash and branch pick-up)
  • Leaf Collection
  • Mini-Dump (Recycling & Trash Center)
  • Shred It & Forget It Program
Household trash is picked up through private trash haulers which residents must retain. The following companies service Piscataway:

  • Grand Sanitation, (908) 222-1566
  • Republic Residential Services, (908) 561-8380
  • Republic Commercial & Industrial Services, (732) 545-8988
  • RGB Disposal
    • Phone: (732) 968-1131
    • Email: info@rgbdisposal.com 
  • Total Trash Removal
    • Phone: (908) 756-3867
    • Email: info@totaltrashremoval.com
  • Waste Management, (908) 879-5616
Please be advised that Piscataway Township does not regulate these companies nor does it endorse any particular company.

Hazardous waste is handled by Middlesex County. You can find more information about acceptable items and the schedule of events by clicking HERE. 

Curbside recycling is performed by the Middlesex County Improvement Authority (MCIA). Please note, household recycling is now single stream meaning all items can be placed together in your new 95-gallon ASL cart.  If you have not yet received your new ASL cart, please continue to use your old bins until the new cart is delivered. However, if ASL carts have been delivered in your neighborhood, you must use it for all recycling material. Anything left outside of the new ASL carts will not be picked up.
