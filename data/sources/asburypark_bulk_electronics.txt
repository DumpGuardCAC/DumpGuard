Bulk Waste, White Goods, Electronics Disposal, Yard Waste, Leaf Collection & More 
 Find information below on bulk waste, white goods, electronics disposal, battery disposal, tire drop-off, yard waste, and leaf collection in Asbury Park. For questions, contact Public Works at 732-775-0900 or report a concern online.
Bulk Waste Collection
Bulk waste is collected on your second garbage day of the week, with a limit of 3 items per pickup. Place items curbside after 5 p.m. the day before collection. To confirm pickup, call Public Works at 732-775-0900.
Accepted Items
Carpeting
Couches
Dressers
Large chairs
Mattresses or box springs (must be wrapped in plastic)
Mirrors
Padding
Rugs
Wooden bed frames
Wooden bookcases
Not accepted: TVs, electronics, white goods, or yard waste.
White Goods Collection
White goods are collected by appointment with a limit of 5 items per pickup. Call Public Works at 732-775-0900 to schedule.
Important: Doors must be removed from refrigerators, freezers, and any other appliances with airtight compartments before placing them curbside. This is required by law and helps prevent accidental entrapment.
Accepted Items
Air conditioners
All metal items
BBQ grills
Freezers
Metal tables
Refrigerators
Stoves
Electronics Disposal
Electronics are not collected curbside. Drop off at the Asbury Park Recycling Center, 9 Main Street (use the Springwood Avenue entrance, across from the Transportation Center).
Accepted Items
TVs, computers, monitors, printers, scanners, copiers
Stereos, VCRs, DVD players
Cell phones, cameras
Wires and cables
Battery Disposal
Batteries are not collected curbside by Asbury Park Public Works. Never throw rechargeable batteries in household trash - they can cause fires in garbage trucks and at waste facilities. Rechargeable batteries contain flammable materials that can be punctured or crushed during collection, triggering dangerous fires that put sanitation workers and equipment at risk. Even batteries that appear dead or damaged can ignite. Always handle rechargeable batteries with care and dispose of them at a proper drop-off location.
Disposal Options
Rechargeable batteries, button batteries, car batteries, and UPS (uninterruptible power supply) batteries can be dropped off for free at the Monmouth County Reclamation Center. Common rechargeable batteries include lithium-ion and NiMH batteries found in laptops, cell phones, tablets, power tools, cordless phones, digital cameras, and rechargeable AA/AAA batteries.
Single-use alkaline and heavy-duty batteries (AA, AAA, 9V, etc.) may be disposed of in household trash. They can also be dropped off for recycling at many electronics and office supply stores, including Staples, Best Buy, and similar retailers.
Tire Drop-Off
Tire drop-off is available to Monmouth County residents (ID required) at the Monmouth County Reclamation Center. Limit of 8 car tires (18 inches or less, no rims). Call 732-683-8686 or visit the Monmouth County Reclamation Center for details.
Please note: Rubber tires are not collected by Asbury Park Public Works. Residents are responsible for disposing of rubber tires on their own.
Yard Waste, Grass Clippings & Quarterly Collection
Yard waste includes brush, shrubs, vines, hedges, and old flowers and plants. Grass, leaves, and dirt are not included.
Place bagged yard waste curbside on your scheduled garbage day
Branches must be tied in bundles (max 60 lbs, 4 feet long)
Tree stumps and rubber tires are not collected
Grass Clippings
Grass clippings are not collected curbside and are not accepted at the Recycling Center. Options include leaving clippings on the lawn, composting, or bringing them to the Monmouth County Reclamation Center (fees apply). Improper disposal may result in fines.
Keep it off the street: Blowing grass or leaves into the street can clog storm drains and cause flooding. When yard waste washes into lakes, it breaks down and fuels algae blooms - which can be harmful to wildlife, fish, and even dogs.
Quarterly Yard Waste Collection
Additional yard waste collection takes place during the weeks of March 31, June 30, September 30, and December 31. Place piles of leaves, branches, plants, vines, and other yard debris curbside for pickup the following week. Soil and grass clippings are not accepted. Do not use plastic bags.
Leaf Collection & Christmas Tree Disposal
Leaf collection runs during two designated periods each year:
Spring: April 1 - May 15
Fall: October 15 - December 31
Place leaves in biodegradable bags or loose piles, leaving a 6-inch gap from the curb. Do not place leaves within 10 feet of storm drains or rake them into the street. Only leaves are accepted - no branches, grass, dirt, or brush. Leaves placed outside of designated collection periods may result in violations.
Christmas Tree Disposal
Christmas trees are collected curbside throughout January. Place trees at the curb without bags, tinsel, or ornaments on your normal garbage collection day. Tree collection ends January 31.
