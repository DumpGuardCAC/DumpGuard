Garbage & Recycling 
 Find information on the City of Asbury Park's garbage and recycling collection program below.
Collection Schedule
Garbage and recycling are picked up by zone. Garbage is collected twice weekly; recycling is collected once weekly. View the collection schedule (PDF).
Containers may be placed curbside after 5 p.m. the day before collection and must be removed by 6 p.m. the day of collection (Ordinance 19-2.1).
Business District trash collection runs Monday, Wednesday, and Friday from May 1 through September 30. Recycling collection remains unchanged for residents in this area.
Recycling Drop-Off
Recycling drop-off is available at the Asbury Park Recycling Center, located at 9 Main Street (use the Springwood Avenue entrance, across from the Asbury Park Transportation Center).
Monday – Friday: 4:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Saturday & Sunday: 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.
Garbage Collection
The following items should be placed in your regular household garbage rather than your recycling bin:
Items soiled with food, human, or pet waste
Ceramics, porcelain, pottery
Coat hangers, toasters, cooking pots
Glass cookware/bakeware, mirrors, microwave trays
Light bulbs
Plastic bags
Unnumbered plastic containers
Window or auto glass
Recycling Collection
Asbury Park uses a single stream recycling system — place glass, plastic, metal, paper, and cardboard together in one bin. Do not bag recyclables.
Dos and Don'ts
Do empty and rinse bottles, jars, and containers
Do remove lids, caps, and straws
Do not exceed 60 lbs per container
Do not bag recyclables — plastic bags clog machinery
What to Recycle and What Not to Recycle
Not sure if something is recyclable? Use the search tool below or review the lists.
Accepted:
Glass bottles and jars — empty, rinsed, caps removed (labels OK)
Metal cans — aluminum, tin, bi-metal, and empty non-hazardous aerosol cans; empty and rinsed (labels OK)
Paper and cardboard — flatten all boxes; newspapers, magazines, softcover books, office paper, paper bags
Plastic containers — #1 and #2 plastics with a pourable neck only; empty, rinsed, lids removed
Not accepted:
Aluminum foil or plates
Broken glass, ceramics, dishes, drinking glasses
Food-soiled or waxed cardboard (e.g., pizza boxes)
Plastic bags, wrap, or unnumbered / #3–7 plastics
Milk or juice cartons, takeout containers
Shredded paper, used tissues or napkins
Styrofoam, cookware, metal scraps, paint cans
