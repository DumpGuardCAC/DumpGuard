Recycling Collection
 How Can You Recycle?
The Township provides a curbside recycling collection program for basic recyclable items (single stream containers, newspaper, magazines, mixed paper and corrugated cardboard). The curbside recycling program is a simple and efficient way residents can properly dispose of recyclables. Recyclables must be placed at your curbside in appropriate recycling containers prior to 6:00 A.M. on your recycling days. Large cardboard is placed next to container.
Furthermore, the Edison Township Recycling Center located at 745 New Durham Rd. is open Monday - Saturday between the hours of 6:30 AM - 2:00 PM. Residents must be able to provide proof of residency (i.e. driver's license or utility bill). Recycling will only be accepted if it has been brought to the center in a residential vehicle. There is staff on site for assistance. Please do not leave any items outside the gate when the Recycling Drop-Off Center is closed. This is a violation of Township Ordinance.
**Security cameras on location for your safety
How To Determine your Recycling Section?
The Township is divided into 10 Recycling Sections (RS). Materials for recycling are collected every other week (bi-weekly). Refer to page 9 for your Recycling Section and Recycling Collection Day. The specific dates for each recycling section are shown on the Month-by-Month Calendar starting on page 10. Multi-family residences have centralized recycling areas from which recyclables are collected bi- weekly. Contact your Management office for recyclable days and centralized area locations.
RECYCLE – IT’S THE LAW! EVERYONE MUST RECYCLE...EVERYONE MUST HAVE SEPARATE RECYCLING AND GARBAGE CONTAINERS…RECYCLABLES MUST NOT BE MIXED WITH HOUSEHOLD GARBAGE....
1.Single Stream Collection Items:
Includes newspapers, magazines, mixed paper, cardboard, food containers, glass bottles/jars, plastic containers (#1 & #2 aluminum, tin, and bi-metal cans.
All items may be mixed together in one container with the exception of cardboard that is too large to fit into the recycling container.
2.Large or CorrugatedCardboard
 Single Family Homes:
* Place next to recyclable containers
* No breaking up necessary
* Do not place Recycling in plastic bags-plastic bags should be thrown away in your garbage
 Multi Family Homes that use a dumpster:
* Break up is necessary 
* Do not place Recycling in plastic bags-plastic bags should be thrown away in your garbage
3. White Goods, Metal/Large Bulky Items
