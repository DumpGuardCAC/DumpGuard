- Curbside: aluminum and steel cans; glass bottles and jars; plastic bottles, jars, jugs, tubs (1 gallon or less); newspaper and mixed paper; corrugated cardboard.
- County programs: Tire Drop-Off (passenger vehicle and light truck tires at designated locations); Paint Drop-Off (latex and oil-based paints and stains through scheduled collection days); Cooking Oil Recycling (used liquid cooking oil at select locations).
- HHW guidelines: keep materials in original containers; no containers over 10 gallons; do not bring electronics and appliances, tires, explosives or ammunition, medical or infectious waste, empty containers; stay in vehicle.
- Paper shredding event: residential personal paper only, no business documents; shredding on site; no plastic bags (box, crate or sturdy container); limit 5 file boxes or 100 lb per vehicle; staples and paper clips can stay; do not bring plastic binders, sheet protectors, newspapers, magazines, hardcover books, junk mail, photos, X-rays, CDs, shredded paper, garbage.
- Bulky waste: contact local public works for curbside bulk pickup; or licensed hauler or private transfer station for a fee.
- Recyclables are not bagged; plastic bags and film should not be included.
