Curbside: aluminum cans; glass bottles and jars; steel cans; plastic bottles, jars, jugs and tubs (1 gallon or less); newspaper; mixed paper (magazines, office paper, junk mail, cereal boxes); corrugated cardboard.
Also must be recycled, not curbside: leaves, brush, motor oil, textiles, masonry, rechargeable batteries (Ni-Cd, Ni-MH, Li-ion, small sealed lead), tires, computers/televisions/monitors/printers/fax machines, white goods.
Woodbridge, Edison, East Brunswick etc.: "should contact their municipal recycling coordinator."

FAQ:
- Shredded paper: not in the curbside bin; trash or backyard compost; use the paper shredding program.
- Recyclables bagged? No - go directly into the curbside bin; no plastic bags/film in the bin.
- Plastic bags at the curb? No - tangle machinery. Reuse them or put them in regular garbage.
- Kiddie pools with #2: trash (too large).
- Frozen food boxes: generally should not be recycled (waxy coating).
- Television: see the Electronics Recycling Program page.
- Bin overflow: buy another bin or use your own can no larger than 32 gallons marked "recycling". Recyclables cannot be put in plastic bags in any town.
- Pizza boxes: most are greasy or contaminated and should not be recycled; ask your municipality about clean ones.
- Egg cartons: plastic or Styrofoam no; pulped paper yes.
