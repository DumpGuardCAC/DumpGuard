 Second Saturday Recycling - Electronics & Paint Products
 Every second Saturday of the month from 8 a.m. to 12 p.m., all City residents can drop off paint and electronics at 400 Jersey Avenue.
Electronics accepted from CITY residents include laptop, desktop and tower computers, monitors, printers, TVs, and fax machines.
Paint products accepted from COUNTY residents include latex paint (water-based), oil-based paint, varnishes, lacquers, thinners, aerosol spray paint, and driveway sealer. Unacceptable paint items include empty containers, and latex paint that is dried out (which can be disposed in regular trash).
All paint items must be labeled.
No paint-related commercial or industrial waste is permitted.
If you miss us on a Second Saturday, please refer to this LIST for alternative drop-off locations on other Saturdays. Items not listed should be taken to a scheduled Middlesex County Household Hazardous Waste Day.
