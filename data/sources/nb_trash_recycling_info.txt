 Trash & Recycling - General Information
RESIDENTIAL RECYCLING is mandatory in the State of New Jersey.
At no time are you permitted to place recyclables in the trash.
TRASH COLLECTION
Municipal Regulations for Solid Waste Collection and Disposal
All bulk item pickup reservations must be made online.
Have something large to throw away in the trash?
Please schedule a bulk pickup.
Examples of items to be discarded: 
-Bulk furniture: mattresses, boxsprings, sofas, tables, dressers
-Metal: washers, dryers, grill, bicycles
-CFC white appliances: refrigerators and air conditioners
-Brush: small branches or twigs from yard cleanups
 2026 Trash and Recycling Collection Schedule - English and Spanish
Download the free RecycleCoach app to learn what’s recyclable and what’s trash. 
Utilize the "What Goes Where" tool. It's as simple as taking a picture of what you want to throw away.
Recyclables
Recyclables will be collected between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM on your zone's assigned dates. 
Recyclables must be placed curbside after 5:00 PM on the evening before the scheduled collection day, or by 6:00 AM on your scheduled day.
Restrictions: 
- Bags CANNOT be used as recycling containers and will not be collected.
- No recyclables will be collected on the holidays as indicated on the Collection Schedule above.
Trash
Household trash must be placed curbside after 5:00 PM on the evening before the scheduled collection day, or by 6:00 AM on your scheduled day.
Restrictions: 
- Trash may also be placed in plastic disposal bags, not less than 3 mils thick, minimum 13 gallon capacity, and securely tied. 
- Up to three (3) trash containers or bags per household unit will be accepted.
- No container or bag may weigh more than 50 lbs.
- Residents must go online to schedule for a collection of bulk items or additional solid waste material. 
- No trash will be collected on the holidays as indicated on the Collection Schedules above.
